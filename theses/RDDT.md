The thesis: Reddit's revenue engine is the logged-in and app cohort, which
is 41% of DAUq (53.5M of 130.3M) and "over 80% of the monetization" (Q3'26
letter). It is not the search-referred logged-out traffic that Google's AI
Mode is removing. Revenue grew 53% with US DAUq down 2%, all of it from
pricing and conversion gains on the engaged base. At $91.66 the market
prices that engine as already breaking. On SBC-charged cash flows the price
returns about the hurdle on a conservative path and ~200bp above it on a
central one. Management has no debt to service and $3.0B of cash with which
to destroy value, and it is buying back stock.

> [!WARNING]
> **Closest attack:** US logged-in DAUq has grown 1% YoY for three straight quarters (10-Q chart) and management concedes the referral loss "flows through to logged-in and App traffic, since search referral is a common source of reengagement" — the cohort carrying the thesis is holding, not growing, and the Q4 low-double-digit total DAUq decline lands on it with unknown leakage.

Load-bearing conditions (bull case):

1. **Revenue growth stays at or above ~25% through 2027 while logged-out
   traffic shrinks**, because logged-in/app users keep holding or growing.
   *plausible*: Q4 is guided +38–42%. Global logged-in is +7% and up QoQ,
   but US logged-in is +1%. The Q4'26 letter (2027-02-04) settles the first
   gate, with logged-in DAUq by geography and the Q1'27 revenue guide.
2. **Ad-stack pricing gains continue.** *probable*: US ARPU went from $9.04
   to $13.82 (+53% YoY). Click volume is up 50%+ and conversion volume more
   than tripled. Scaled-channel revenue is up 80%+ and mid-market advertisers
   up 50%+. Pricing outpaced impressions (Q3'26 call).
3. **The logged-in-only transition is net-neutral or better for
   monetization.** It converts enough logged-out visitors that the lost reach

   *plausible*: every major ad peer already gates. The test starts in
   select non-US markets in Q4, and the Q4'26 call (2027-02-04) is the first
   place results can be discussed.
4. **Margin and SBC discipline holds.** *probable*: adjusted EBITDA margin

   related taxes are 15.2% of revenue (−80bp YoY). FDSO is 206.3M, down
   0.3% QoQ after $100M of buybacks.

a condition. It is the §4 bear-row input, and the condition it hides (#1) is
