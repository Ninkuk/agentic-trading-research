## 2. Business

**Created:** a lender can sell a low-down-payment (<20%) conforming loan to
Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac only with credit enhancement — the GSE charter
requires it — and private MI is the cheapest standing form of it. The
borrower gets a home years before saving 20%; the lender gets a saleable
loan; the GSE gets first-loss cover on the riskiest slice (10-K 2025:
risk-weighted FICO 746, LTV 93%).

**Captured:** there are three mechanisms.
- **Monthly premiums on the in-force book.** Q2 net earned premiums were
  $245M, flat YoY, on $274B primary IIF; 96% of NIW is monthly-premium.
  The revenue is an annuity whose length is persistency (80%). That length
  is exactly what the October order targets on future loans.
- **Investment income on float and capital.** $73M in Q2, up from $66M a
  year earlier. All recent revenue growth comes from here.
- **Risk-based pricing spread.** Premium minus claims minus a ~21% expense
  ratio. CRT (quota share, ILNs) cedes part of the tail. The 2026-10-01
  forward quota share already covers ~35% of a portion of the 2028 book.

**Protected:** regulation, not product. Writing GSE-eligible MI requires
PMIERs approval plus state licensing, and six approved writers exist. Share
among the six is contestable through rate engines, so the base premium rate
drifts down, but entry is not contestable. The threat is substitution and
policy: GSE alternative credit enhancement, lender self-insurance, or
regulator-set coverage and cancellation rules (10-K Item 1A). The moat is
exactly as wide as FHFA lets it be. October showed FHFA and the White House
are now actively narrowing the *duration* of the annuity on future loans.
They have not touched entry, coverage levels or premium rates.

**Control:** one share class. Genworth Financial owns ~81.6% (10-K 2025) and
participates pro rata in buybacks. Enact is a controlled company, so there
is no unsolicited-takeover or activist path through the minority. Genworth
CEO Tom McInerney retires 2026-12-31 and is leaving Enact's board the same
day, "not the result of any dispute or disagreement" (Enact 8-K item 5.02,
filed 2026-10-30). CFO Jerome Upton becomes Genworth CEO on 2027-01-01
(Genworth 8-K, 2026-10-30). Genworth still depends on Enact's cash, which
keeps it aligned with high payouts.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative** since 2022. Revenue growth is
investment income, premiums are flat, and 2022's operating income was
inflated by reserve releases. Q3 is not yet reported, so TTM is still
through 2026-06.

## 3. Threads pulled

  The order is "Decreasing Housing Costs for the American People." The

  have satisfied their equity requirements … based on the current market

## 5. Falsifiers

  structural cut to in-force economics beyond the policy-erosion row.

  above 1.6%: releases have stopped. Revalue on the floor rows, which sit

  distributable base down.

  the policy-erosion row on the stated terms.
- **Shift —** a Genworth-led take-private or stake sale, possibly under the
  new CEO. Revalue to the offer; a bid below book ($39.06) harms minority
