## 2. Business

Unchanged from `research/GOOG-2026-10-29.md` §2 (Created / Captured /

**Created:** the fastest route from a question to an answer at zero price
for users; intent for advertisers; and a vertically integrated AI stack for
enterprises (TPU, Gemini, Vertex, Wiz, Workspace), now including whole TPU
systems. First-party model APIs process nearly 32B tokens per minute, more

**Captured:** (a) Search & other auctions $63,350M; (b) YouTube ads

hardware (8-K Ex. 99.1; 10-Q commitments note). TAC $16,366M.

**Protected:** Search rests on a query-training-advertiser flywheel plus
distribution, now under a behavioral data-sharing order. Cloud rests on
cost per token from owned silicon and on contracted backlog. Neither
forecloses a frontier rival being simply better, and selling TPUs
deliberately strengthens competitors in exchange for scale. New launches on
Nov 9 (two more TPU systems, four this year) add to the claim but not to the
disclosure of what the silicon earns.

**Control:** Three classes. Class B (10 votes) 828M shares with Page and
Brin holding a voting majority (10-K FY2025 Item 1A, read in earlier runs);
Class A (this ticker) 5,888M shares, one vote; Class C 5,578M, no vote (8-K
Ex. 99.1). The founders foreclose an unsolicited takeover or activist path.
GOOGL's vote carries no control value in practice: it closed 0.75% above
GOOG ($286.01) on 11-09.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive.** Revenue FY2021→TTM $257.6B →
$471.1B (+83%); operating income $78.7B → $157.0B (+99%); margin 30.6% →
33.3%. FY2022 $282.8B / $74.8B; FY2023 $307.4B / $84.3B; FY2024 $350.0B /
$112.4B; FY2025 $402.8B / $129.0B (stockanalysis income statement, annual;
