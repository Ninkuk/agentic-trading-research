## 2. Business

**Created:** As filed today, Kaixin is a Cayman holding company whose H1 2026
revenue was $667K. Of that, $648K was new-car wholesale through Jet Sound
Hong Kong and $19K was EV design consulting (H1 2026 Ex. 99.1, 6-K
2026-09-16). The customer gets a car sourced through a Hong Kong wholesaler.
Nothing filed says why they would buy here rather than from any other parallel
importer or dealer. No vehicle count is disclosed. The Robinhood profile shows
13 employees. The FY2021 business (dealership network and used cars, $253.8M
revenue) is gone: Kaixin Auto Group was disposed of in June 2024 because of
negative net assets of $13.6M, and Kaixin Daman and Kaixin Jingtao were
disposed of "due to minimal operations" (20-F FY2025).

**Captured:** One mechanism: the gross spread on wholesale cars. In H1 2026
that was $23K on $667K (3.4%). The company's own history at scale is worse:
$1.04M of gross profit on $253.8M of FY2021 revenue (0.4%), and −$22.3M on
$82.8M in FY2022 (stockanalysis financials). Consulting is a rounding error.
The 2025 announcements of a digital-asset strategy (2025-10-06), XINGCAN AI
education (2025-09-10) and Honglu AI animation (term sheet, Oct-2025) have no
revenue line in the H1 2026 statements.

**Protected:** No moat. Car wholesale is a price-taker business with no
switching cost, and nothing in the filings describes exclusivity, a brand
franchise or a supply contract that stops a competitor. That silence is the
answer.

**Control:** Dual class. Class B carries 250 votes per share. At YE2025, CEO
Mingjun Lin held 52.9% and CFO Yi Yang 43.3% of total voting power (20-F
FY2025). Class B rose from 144,667 shares (2026-06-30) to 3,144,668
(2026-09-16), which at 250 votes is ~786M votes against 42.1M Class A votes.
The insiders' control is near-total. Who received the new 3M Class B is not
in the documents read. The escrowed acquisition sellers vote their 34M Class A
shares during escrow (SPA Art. 3.2). This forecloses any takeover and any
activist path, and the minority holder has no say on issuance.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue collapsed by more than
