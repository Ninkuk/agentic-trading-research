bexobrutideg (NX-5948), destroys the BTK protein instead of blocking it, and

toxicities (8-K 2026-10-13). Roche paid $700M upfront in June 2026, funds 60%
of development, and splits US profits 50/50. The company is persistently
loss-making apart from that one upfront, which is the Phase 0 fast-kill
category. Judgment call for an unattended run: continued past Phase 0
because net cash covers 59% of the market cap and the 38% one-day drop on
11-04 (Sanofi dropping a partnered STAT6 degrader) made the price worth
checking. The pass rests on price. BeOne's rival degrader has already met its

## 2. Business

**Created:** a degrader removes the target protein, so it works against
the resistance mutations that defeat covalent BTK inhibitors (C481) and
non-covalent ones (pirtobrutinib), and it also removes BTK's scaffolding
function. The CEO says ~40% of relapsed patients carry BTK mutations (Morgan
Stanley conference 2026-09-16, primary-transcribed via stockanalysis).
Patients who have cycled through covalent BTKi, BCL2i, and pirtobrutinib have
few options left, and that is the 3L+ population CLL-201 enrolls. The
customer surplus is real if the Phase 1 holds: 83% ORR in a heavily
pretreated population. Two further indications, multiple sclerosis (CSF:plasma
~1:1, per the CEO) and chronic spontaneous urticaria, are pre-Phase 2 option
value.

**Captured:** four distinct mechanisms, none yet recurring:
(a) partner upfronts and milestones: Roche $700M upfront plus ~$1.6B in
milestones split roughly evenly across clinical, regulatory, and sales;
Gilead (IRAK4 GS-6791, research extensions, $10M in FQ3); Pfizer ($7M
milestone in FQ3); Sanofi (~$139M cumulative, with ~$455M in remaining
STAT6 milestones now forfeited). (b) 50% of US bexobrutideg profit after
paying 40% of development. (c) tiered ex-US royalties from Roche, rates
undisclosed. (d) wholly owned assets: NX-1607, an oral CBL-B inhibitor in
Phase 1b with ~30-patient cohorts. Sources: 8-K 2026-10-13, Morgan Stanley
conference 2026-09-16.

**Protected:** composition-of-matter patents plus the DEL-AI discovery
platform. The platform is a capability, not a moat: BeOne reached the same
target with a similar profile first ("the efficacy data does look similar").
The protection that matters is the bexobrutideg patent estate and its expiry,
which this run did not read. Against BeOne specifically there is no
mechanism that stops it; the claimed edge is proteomic selectivity, which is
domain science this run cannot evaluate (UNKNOWN).

**Control:** one listed class of common stock (Nasdaq: NRIX). No
controlling holder was identified; institutions hold 82.4% and insiders 0.89%
(`stocks.db`). The proxy was not read, so >5% holders and any charter
