## 2. Business

**Created:** Two distinct customers. International (59% of nine-month direct
premiums) sells US-dollar whole life and endowment policies to non-US
residents in 80+ countries, chiefly Colombia, Ecuador, Argentina, Taiwan and
Venezuela. The customer gets a dollar asset outside a local currency and
banking system, which is the surplus. Domestic (41%) sells simplified-issue
final-expense whole life, averaging about $8,000 of face, through 5,000-plus
appointed independent agents in 43 states.

**Captured:** Three mechanisms. Mortality spread on premiums ($128.4M YTD net);
investment spread on a $1.4B, A-rated, 88% fixed-maturity book at a 4.48%
yield ($52.4M YTD); and small policy fees and supplemental-contract income,
which management expects to shrink as endowments mature. There is no fee or
asset-spread business of scale; the CEO said at LD Micro that revenue is
"pretty focused on mortality spread."

**Protected:** Internationally, a real but narrow mechanism: decades of
relationships with independent agencies in markets where a US-dollar policy
from a US insurer is hard to buy, plus licensing and language. It is not
exclusive: the 10-K names foreign insurers and local carriers as competitors,
and Taiwan sales are falling on a distributor succession problem. Domestically
there is no moat. The 10-K calls final expense fragmented with no dominant
competitor, the product is commoditized by agent commission, and the company
needed RGA to take 50% of new business to afford the growth.

**Control:** One share, one vote, no controller. Class A only; 2,000,000
Class B shares are authorized with the exclusive right to elect a simple
majority of the board, but none are outstanding. Holders at 5%+ per the
2026 proxy: Galindo, Arias & Lopez as trustee of four non-US trusts (8.2%),
BlackRock (5.8%), Continental General / Michael Gorzynski (5.0%, passive
13G filed 2026-03-25). Directors and officers hold 2.2%. Nothing forecloses
a takeover or an activist.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue up 2.7% from FY21 to TTM
while operating income fell 77%.
