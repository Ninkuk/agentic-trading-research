## 2. Business

**Created:** Corcept sells cortisol-pathway drugs into two underserved groups.
Korlym and its authorized generic treat hypercortisolism, a disease
endocrinologists under-diagnose; CATALYST found it in 23.8% of 1,057
difficult-to-control diabetics (10-K). Lifyorli (relacorilant plus
nab-paclitaxel) added a median 4.1 months of survival in platinum-resistant
ovarian cancer, 16.0 vs 11.9 months, HR 0.65 (ROSELLA, Lancet 2026). The
customer gets a treatment option where few exist, and the surplus is real.

**Captured:** Three mechanisms. (1) Korlym brand plus authorized generic, sold
through one exclusive specialty pharmacy; Curant collects more than 99% of
hypercortisolism revenue (10-Q). Price contributed 24.8% of Q3 Korlym growth
via an August 2025 increase and better reimbursement, offset by mix toward the
cheaper AG. (2) Lifyorli at a $37,900 WAC per 28-day cycle (Corcept state price
disclosure), launched 2026-04-01, through specialty pharmacy and distributors.
(3) Gross margin near 98.5% across both; Medicaid pays a much lower price, and
accrued government rebates rose to $52.5M from $35.7M at year-end.

**Protected:** For Korlym, not patents. The composition patent has expired, and
the Federal Circuit affirmed that Teva does not infringe the '214 and '800
method patents; en banc rehearing was denied 2026-07-10. What actually protects
Korlym is the distribution and reimbursement arrangement, which is the conduct
under attack from Teva and from Aetna, HCSC, Humana and Molina. **The moat is
the defendant.** Relacorilant, if approved, is the patent-protected successor
(hypercortisolism patents 2033–2040, seven-year orphan exclusivity). For
Lifyorli, oncology method patents run 2033–2042, and the mechanism is genuinely
different from an ADC. Whether that defends share against a better drug is
UNKNOWN. The relacorilant science is domain-specific; I can read trial
endpoints but cannot evaluate the GR-modulation mechanism itself.

**Control:** One share class, no controlling holder, annual board elections per
the 2026 proxy. BlackRock 10.7%, Vanguard 8.9%, Ingalls & Snyder 7.6%,
Renaissance 5.4%, Belanoff 6.1% including 3.83M options; directors and officers
20.7%. Nothing structural forecloses a takeover or an activist in the portion
of the proxy read; a poison pill was not seen there.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue rose 2.2× across FY20–25
while operating income fell 65%; the 2026 quarters are inflecting positive.
