## 2. Business

**Created:** Hainan Kylin (51% owned) runs livestream e-commerce services for
vendors on Chinese short-video platforms, charging advance service fees of
roughly 15–30% of retail price (424B5 2026-09-15). Antelope Chengdu sells
business-management and information-systems consulting ($0.5M in six months).
The customer buys traffic and selling capacity, and the surplus looks thin:
active clients fell by about 44 to just over 133, and the top five produced
$18.8M of $29.4M (MD&A 2026-06-30). A former ceramic-tile maker, the company
sold that business in April 2023 (20-F).

**Captured:** Three mechanisms, only one of them material.
- Service spread on livestream volume: $29.4M revenue, about $0.8M gross
  profit, 2.85% margin (2.12% a year earlier).
- Consulting fees: $0.5M revenue, about $0.4M gross profit.
- Planned and unproven: selling Midland, Texas gas-fired power and hosting
  crypto miners through AEHL US. Revenue to date: none.
Non-operating items did much of the work in the half: other income of $2.1M
(a $1.2M grant, $0.7M loan forgiveness) exceeded gross profit of $1.3M. Hainan
Kylin's 49% minority took $0.7M of profit while the parent shareholders lost
$3.3M.

**Protected:** No moat. Management itself attributes the revenue fall to a
traffic-centralisation change that lost customers, and cites "fierce
competition" and lower service rates. Nothing stops a rival doing this
tomorrow. The company ceased DOU+ advertising entirely in 2025, which was
$25.3M of revenue in the nine months of 2024 (20-F), so the platform can remove
a revenue line unilaterally.

**Control:** Dual class; Class B carries 20 votes. The transition 20-F names
Weilai (Will) Zhang, former CEO and chairman and father of CEO Tingting Zhang,

and the company is a Nasdaq "controlled company." Filings now show 2,555,497

unchanged across the 1-for-6 and 1-for-16 splits, both described as Class A
splits, so each split raised Class B's relative weight (inference; charter not
read). This forecloses a hostile bid or activist path. The company also owes
Mr. Zhang and Baiya International Group, a major shareholder, $1.18M on demand.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue grew 5.5× from FY2021 to
