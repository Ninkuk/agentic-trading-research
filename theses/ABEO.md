current launch slope the business breaks even only around 2029, and once the

## 2. Business

**Created:** RDEB patients carry chronic open wounds that never close.
ZEVASKYN (prademagene zamikeracel) takes a skin biopsy, inserts a working
COL7A1 gene into the patient's own keratinocytes, grows them into sheets in
Cleveland, and grafts up to twelve sheets onto the worst wounds in one
surgical session. Large chronic wounds are where topical alternatives do
least, and a single procedure that durably closes them is worth a lot to
patients and payers (company frames lifetime RDEB cost at $10–20M; MedCity /
PSG coverage, low-confidence). The surplus is real if the grafts hold. The
company says durability is the core claim; this run cannot evaluate the
dermatology, and it is UNKNOWN beyond the label.

**Captured:** three mechanisms, one recurring:
(a) a one-time product sale per treatment at a $3.1M list price
(pharmaphorum, 2025 approval coverage), net ~$2.7M recognised per billed
treatment in Q3 ($13.491M / 5 billed). Revenue is booked only when a
treatment ships at least four sheets and passes release (Q3 call). Outcomes-
based rebates and subsequent-treatment discounts are carved out as a material
right (10-Q Q3 2026: $3.26M allowances, $0.68M deferred). (b) Tiered
royalties of up to 8% plus commercial milestones on Ultragenyx's FAYUVI
(UX111, MPS IIIA), out-licensed in 2022 and FDA-approved 2026-09-18 (Q3 PR).
(c) ABO-801 (ex-APT-1011, oral fluticasone for EoE): no revenue before a
2029+ approval, and if approved it owes Ellodi $30M on approval, up to $30M
in sales milestones and low-to-mid-teens royalties (8-K 2026-11-09).

**Protected:** regulatory exclusivity plus manufacturing difficulty. As a
BLA-approved biologic it carries 12 years of data exclusivity from April 2025
(statutory, not company-stated) plus orphan exclusivity. The process (84-hour
shelf life, autologous sheets, a GMP facility, qualified treatment centres)
is hard to copy, which is a mechanism. What it does not protect against is
substitution. Krystal's VYJUVEK, a re-dosable topical gene therapy for the
same DEB wounds, sold $95.3M in the US in Q3 2026 alone (Krystal 8-K Q3
2026). ZEVASKYN's market is the subset of wounds VYJUVEK does not close, and
its size is UNKNOWN.

**Control:** one class of common stock, no controlling holder. Insiders hold
17.4% and institutions 50.4% (`stocks.db`); the CEO holds 1,286,500 shares
(Form 4 2026-09-29). The James Russell Revocable Trust holds 3,479,963 shares
(6.1%) and turned activist by 13D on 2026-10-23. It cites execution and
statements that do not match results, asks for added independent directors
and a leadership review, and reserves nominations for the 2027 annual
meeting. The 11-06 issuances (4.49M Ellodi shares, 8.0M PIPE shares and
pre-funded warrants) dilute that stake toward ~4.7%. At the 2026 AGM,
shareholders passed every item except removing the advance-notice
requirement for director nominations (AGM 2026-06-12, stockanalysis summary,
low-confidence), so an activist slate must clear advance notice. The proxy
was not read for other charter defences.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative on the annual record, turning
positive across the four launch quarters.** Stockanalysis folds R&D into cost
of revenue; operating income is the comparable line.
