## 2. Business

Unchanged from `MMS-2026-09-08.md` §2 except for the Agile delta below.

**Created:** governments get citizen-facing operations they must run but
cannot staff or scale. That covers the 1-800-MEDICARE and marketplace contact
centers, Medicaid/SNAP eligibility processing, clinical assessments and
appeals, and VA compensation-claim medical exams, all bought as measured,
surge-capable accountability (10-K Item 1). Agile adds embedded engineering
for DoD/DHS customers (CDAO, DISA, NORTHCOM/NORAD AI work, USCIS): engineers
deployed alongside the users rather than software handed over (deal call).

**Captured:** (i) per-transaction and milestone fees on performance-based
contracts, 54% of revenue, with incentive/disincentive overlays; (ii)
fixed-price work, 13%; (iii) cost-plus and T&M for the rest. Segments: U.S.
Federal 56% at a 16.5–17% margin, U.S. Services 32% at 9.5–10%, Outside U.S.
11% at breakeven (10-K; Q3 release). Agile, about $450M of CY27 revenue, is
>50% fixed-price and ~6% cost-plus at a 12–15% adjusted EBITDA margin. Its
largest contract is ~$50M a year and its top ten are just under half of
revenue (deal call). MDE-NG changes how the VA leg captures value: volume
becomes a ranking-based share instead of a regional franchise.

**Protected:** incumbency where switching costs are political as well as
operational, plus VES's booked clinician network. This protects nothing
against the customer itself. The VA paused incentives unilaterally, and
MDE-NG now writes vendor substitution into the contract: each group must
have two awardees able to absorb half its volume within 30 days of award.
Under that design the VA stops paying for switching costs. What remains is
performance rank, which Maximus says it leads on (deal call). That claim is
unverified.

**Control:** one class of common, one vote per share, no controlling holder.
The largest holders are BlackRock and Vanguard (13G/As), and insiders own
1.44% (stocks.db). Nothing forecloses a takeover or an activist. PSUs pay on
organic growth, adjusted EBITDA margin and FCF (DEF 14A 2026-01-21).
Acquired revenue does not count toward the organic-growth PSU, so the deal
was not bought to hit the comp target.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): positive on a lower base.** Revenue $4.25B
(FY21) → $4.63B → $4.90B → $5.31B → $5.43B (FY25) → $5.25B TTM; operating
income $418M → $315M → $295M → $488M → $528M → $579M TTM (stocks.db TTM
opInc $578,987,000; no quarter filed since 6/30). Operating income has
outgrown revenue since FY23 even as revenue fell in the TTM.
