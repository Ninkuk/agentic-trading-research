## 2. Business

**Created:** Two unrelated things. (a) Furniture: sofas, beds, dining and
cabinet lines under Nova LifeStyle / Diamond Sofa / Nova Living, sourced from
Asian manufacturers and sold to US retailers and distributors. The customer
gets mid-market furniture with nothing that cannot be had from many other
importers. (b) "API-based services": cloud inference APIs / model-as-a-
service, started Q2 2026 ($1.10M revenue in its first quarter). The filings
reviewed do not describe what the customer gets that a hyperscaler or a
neocloud does not also offer.

**Captured:** (a) Wholesale furniture margin: 22.9% TTM gross margin
(stockanalysis) on a declining base. Furniture sales fell 34% in H1 2026
($3.42M vs $5.19M, 10-Q). (b) Resale of rented compute, priced per API call
or per GPU-hour. The first quarter captured nothing, with cost of sales above
sales. From 2026-10-29 the company is billed ~$2.04M/month by DSH Hyper (on
kt cloud capacity), whether or not it finds customers. (c) In practice the
equity's value capture runs through (d): marking up private stakes (Preamble
fund 2.36× cost in one half-year) and booking the gain through "other
income".

**Protected:** No moat. Furniture importing has no switching costs, and the
company has shrunk for years. Reselling another provider's rented B300
capacity on a 60-month take-or-pay is the opposite of a moat: the lessor
(kt cloud via DSH) holds the scarce asset, and XMax holds the fixed
liability. The silence is the answer.

**Control:** One class of common stock, one vote per share, no preferred
described (DEF 14A 2026-10-05). The beneficial-ownership table lists **no
holdings for any of the 7 directors and officers and no 5% holder**, out of
64,875,626 shares. Quorum is set at one-third. Control in practice sits with
whoever buys the Reg S placements. The proxy itself warns a large placement
purchaser could concentrate voting power. Nothing forecloses a takeover or
an activist, but nobody inside has a stake aligned with common holders.

**Operating leverage (Phase 0): negative.** Revenue went sideways while
operating income never turned positive. The TTM loss widened as the AI
